### SurferSEO: Making SEO Better
Using SurferSEO has become an essential part of my daily routine to generate AI content for clients, where appropriate for a particular industry. It generates content based on the top 10 ranking pages for a defined group of keywords/keyphrases, so it is specifically using current high-ranking pages that are successful in Google search as its source.&nbsp;

However, one of SurferSEO's best features is that it allows you to edit the AI-generated content on screen as it analyzes it in real time, so that you can produce even better content with human input, especially in the area of Natural Language Processing (NLP), which is part of Google's search algorithm. The AI-generated content also uses the optimum number of paragraphs and headers.&nbsp;

Plus, it includes image suggestions from copyright-free sites, such as Pixabay, that apply to the content being produced. So, it not only saves time on the content creation but also on sourcing relevant images.
