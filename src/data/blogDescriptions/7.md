#### **Tailor Deck to Venture Capitalist Needs**
Building the perfect pitch deck to raise funding from venture capital requires understanding what venture capitalists are looking for and tailoring your pitch deck to meet those needs.&nbsp;

One of the most important things to include in your pitch deck is a clear and concise overview of your business model. This should include information about your target market, your competitive advantage, and your growth strategy. It should also include information about how you plan to use the funding you receive from venture capital.&nbsp;

Venture capitalists want to know that their investment will help your business grow and that you have a clear plan for how to achieve that growth. Including this information in your pitch deck will help you demonstrate that you have a solid plan in place and that you are a good investment.

#### **Support Claims with Solid Evidence**
Building the perfect pitch deck is all about backing up your claims and expectations with evidence. Sure, you also want it to look good and be structured well, but at the end of the day, you won't raise funding if you can't back up your claims with real numbers.&nbsp;

So, when building a pitch deck, think about every goal you set and every claim you make. Then, when you mention that goal or claim in your pitch deck, also include the information that backs that up and shows potential investors that you are already showing promising signs of achieving those goals and moving towards success.

As I already said, it's all about being able to back yourself up with evidence. Anyone can make a claim that they're going to be the best business in their industry, but very few can actually show that they're going to be the best.



[Lauren Carlstrom](https://www.linkedin.com/in/laurencarlstrom/), COO, [Oxygen Plus](https://www.oxygenplus.com)



#### **Grip Attention and Gain Trust**
Constructing an ideal pitch deck for venture capital funding is like walking a tightrope—you need to simultaneously grip their attention and gain their trust. It's about kindling their excitement with your vision, but also demonstrating that you've done your homework with impeccable research.&nbsp;

Clearly communicate the problem, your solution, and how you stand out from competitors. Most importantly, remember to highlight your team's capabilities. Investors don't just invest in ideas, they invest in the people who bring them to life.



[Abid Salahi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/abid-salahi/), Co-Founder and CEO, [FinlyWealth](https://www.finlywealth.com)



#### **Blend Storytelling with Empirical Evidence**
Crafting a compelling pitch deck is a blend of storytelling and empirical evidence. From my experience, the most effective pitch decks are those that articulate a clear problem, offer a unique solution, and present compelling market data.&nbsp;

My top recommendation would be to focus on the “why” behind your product or solution. Start with a captivating narrative that highlights the pain points of the target market. Then, provide robust data that showcases the market size and potential growth. Wrap it up with a crisp outline of your business model, traction, and financial projections.&nbsp;

It's essential to be succinct, visually engaging, and tailored to your audience. In my experience, a pitch deck isn't just about showcasing your business; it's about building a genuine connection with your potential investors.



[Ankit Prakash](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ankitprakash/), Founder, [Sprout24](https://sprout24.com)



#### **Keep It Concise, Focus on Key Aspects**
To build the perfect pitch deck for venture capital funding, my top tip is to keep it concise and compelling. Focus on highlighting the problem your product or service solves, the market opportunity, your unique value proposition, and the traction you've gained.&nbsp;

Be clear, use visuals effectively, and tell a compelling story. Investors receive numerous pitches, so a concise and engaging presentation will capture their attention and leave a lasting impression.



[Ben Seelen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/benseelen), Founder, [Rugby Brix](https://rugbybricks.com)



#### **Add Credibility with Testimonials**
Including testimonials or success stories from early customers or partners in your pitch deck adds credibility and showcases the value of your product or service. Testimonials provide evidence that you have a viable solution and help establish trust with venture capital investors.&nbsp;

For example, you can include quotes or case studies from satisfied customers who have benefited from your product, highlighting specific outcomes or advantages they experienced. This not only validates your offering but also demonstrates that you have a track record of delivering value to customers.



[Roy Lau](https://www.linkedin.com/in/roylau0104/), Co-Founder, [28 Mortgage](https://28mortgage.com.hk)



#### **Balance Information and Showcase the Team**
I'm not sure there's such a thing as a perfect pitch deck, because realistically, every investor is looking for and prioritizing different pieces of information for making an investment.&nbsp;

I think sometimes focusing on getting it "perfect" leads people to add more and more information into the deck.&nbsp;

If you're trying to think of everything that any person might ask, you'll end up with a deck that's too long and too dense, and then you lose people.&nbsp;

I tend to recommend that people keep the pages clean—put enough information for each topic that they get the point, but not so much information that they have no questions.&nbsp;

Lastly, people tend to invest in people more than anything. Don't forget to put in information about the team—headshots, their experience, what they bring to the table.&nbsp;

Most people have financial advisors who are going to dig into the numbers; you just need to give someone confidence in the team and the business model to get the ball rolling.


[Katherine Lagow](https://www.linkedin.com/in/katherine-lagow-357528131/), VP of Operations, [FleetNow](https://buyfleetnow.com)



#### **Highlight Company Expertise**
Investors bet on the jockey, not just the horse. Set aside a space to introduce your team and highlight your skills. Highlight significant team members' accomplishments, industry experience, and corporate roles. Show that your team is capable of carrying out the vision and successfully navigating hurdles.



[Cindi Keller](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cindi914/), Communications Coordinator, [The Criminal Defense Firm](https://criminaldefense.com)



#### **Show Resilience through Past Failures**
In your pitch deck, go beyond showcasing your successes and highlight the valuable lessons you've learned from previous failures. This demonstrates resilience, adaptability, and a growth mindset. By openly addressing past challenges, you build trust and show potential investors that you can navigate obstacles effectively.&nbsp;

For example, you can discuss how a previous product iteration failed to gain traction but led to crucial insights that shaped your current successful solution. This highlights your ability to learn from mistakes and adapt your approach, making your venture more attractive to investors.
