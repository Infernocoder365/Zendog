### Introduction
In the world of professional networking, LinkedIn has emerged as the go-to platform for companies and individuals alike. For companies, establishing a strong company page is crucial as it serves as a form of social validation and credibility for customers, employees, and potential investors. However, growing a company page on LinkedIn can be challenging, especially in comparison to personal profiles, which tend to receive more organic exposure. In this blog post, we will delve into effective strategies for boosting your company page's growth on LinkedIn, exploring practical tips and real-life case studies.

### The Challenge of Growing a Company Page on LinkedIn
LinkedIn has a preference for giving free impressions to personal profiles rather than company pages. This is because individuals on the network are more inclined to follow people rather than companies. Personal profiles seem more relatable and less commercial. As a result, gaining traction for company pages requires a more deliberate approach. LinkedIn, like other social media platforms, also encourages businesses to pay for impressions because their advertising platforms are a major revenue driver, making it even more important to explore strategies that can help you increase your reach without solely relying on advertising.

### Tips for Gaining Traction on LinkedIn
1. **Utilize the Invitation Feature**  
   One of the most accessible methods for growing your company page on LinkedIn is by using the invitation feature. Admins of company pages can invite up to 200 of their connections each month. Consistently leveraging this feature ensures that your company page will add new followers at a consistent rate over time. While 200 invitations per month isn’t a lot, it can lead to 30-50 new followers monthly, especially if your page is still small. If someone accepts your invitation, LinkedIn credits you with additional invitations, enabling you to continue inviting more people.

   If you exhaust your existing connections or want to reach a broader audience, consider using tools like Walexy or Alfred, which automate the invitation process. These tools allow you to send invitations to people in your target audience. While some offer free versions, paid plans provide more extensive reach, allowing you to connect with more people daily. By the end of the month, you will have a pool of 200 new connections to invite to follow your company page.

   Additionally, make your team members admins of your corporate page so they can invite their networks. This is particularly helpful if you have key opinion leaders and domain experts on your team who can invite valuable business contacts to follow your page.

2. **Post Job Opportunities**  
   Posting job opportunities on LinkedIn is one of the most effective strategies for gaining traction and increasing followers for your LinkedIn business page. Many users follow company pages when they submit job applications. LinkedIn has added a feature in the application process that encourages users to "Follow the company to stay up to date," which is turned on by default. You can also post jobs at various seniority levels to attract relevant followers.

3. **Experiment with Engaging Content**  
   Merely posting plain or basic content on your company page won't garner attention or engagement, especially if your follower count is low. To effectively leverage content on your LinkedIn company page, consider the following strategies:
   - **Link other companies:** Share updates that link to relevant companies or startups. For example, create a weekly summary of startups that have raised capital and provide links to their profiles. This encourages companies mentioned in your posts to engage with and potentially share your content.
   - **Build a community:** Encourage your team members, including the CEO, to post about the company and share relevant content. By establishing a community around your company and its key individuals, you create a cycle where each team member's content reinforces the visibility and credibility of your company page.
   - **Engage with others:** Take the initiative to engage with content from other LinkedIn users. Commenting on posts by industry thought leaders, customers, or partners can help raise your company's profile and generate interest from others in your network.

### Case Studies: Successful LinkedIn Company Pages That Leverage Engaging Content
Let's examine a few notable case studies of companies that have successfully used the tactics mentioned above:
- **Funden:** This investor matchmaking platform has achieved considerable success on LinkedIn, with nearly 4,000 followers. Their strategy includes posting weekly summaries of startups that have raised capital, linking to those startups' profiles. This not only provides value to their target audience but also encourages sharing and engagement from the featured companies.
- **Refine Labs:** Led by CEO Chris Walker, Refine Labs has built a thriving community on LinkedIn. By posting content personally and encouraging the entire team to share company-related content, they have created a robust content distribution network that amplifies reach and fosters engagement.
- **Apollo:** Apollo effectively utilizes their LinkedIn company page to engage with other users' content. By commenting on relevant posts and sharing creative content, they showcase their industry expertise and generate interest from their target audience.

### Additional Strategies to Consider
1. **Boosting Content with Ad Spend**  
   If you have the budget, consider boosting your best-performing posts through targeted ad campaigns. LinkedIn encourages companies to invest in advertising, and this strategy can help increase your visibility and follower count.

2. **Driving Outside Traffic to LinkedIn**  
   Leverage your established presence on other social media platforms by including links to your LinkedIn company page in your bio. Directing your existing audience to follow you on LinkedIn can help expand your reach.

3. **Cautionary Note on Buying Followers**  
   While it's tempting to purchase followers to inflate your follower count, excessive reliance on bought followers can be detrimental. It's advisable to use bought followers sparingly to give your profile an initial boost, but focus on organic growth and engagement for long-term success.

### Conclusion
Growing a company page on LinkedIn requires a strategic, persistent, and patient approach. By utilizing features like invitations, posting job opportunities, and experimenting with engaging content, you can increase your page's visibility and follower count. Learning from successful case studies can provide valuable insights and inspiration for your LinkedIn growth strategy. It's crucial to remain authentic and prioritize organic growth and engagement to build a reputable and valuable LinkedIn presence.
