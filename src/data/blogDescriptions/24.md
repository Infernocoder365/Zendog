# Introduction
When it comes to website optimization and digital marketing, backlinks play a crucial role. Acting as signals of trust, these simple yet powerful links greatly influence your website's domain authority. Essentially, when other reputable websites link back to your site, search engines like Google perceive your site as trustworthy.

**Why backlinks matter**  
The significance of backlinks extends beyond trust. They serve as a vote of confidence from other sites, reinforcing the value and reliability of your content. The more high-quality backlinks you garner, especially from high-traffic, high-domain-authority sites, the better your content ranks in search engine results. These links act as proof of your website's value, enhancing your online visibility.

**The importance of backlinks in domain authority**  
A higher domain authority, driven by quality backlinks, translates into a favorable position in search results. This increased visibility can organically attract more visitors to your site, fostering business growth by potentially boosting sales or customer engagement.

**The business impact**  
Ranking high in search results is a significant business driver as it can yield traffic without the cost associated with paid methods. Acquiring traffic through methods like Google search ads could cost anywhere from five USD to 500 USD per click, based on the competitiveness of the targeted keywords. Therefore, organic traffic, fueled by a high domain authority, is vital for businesses as it eliminates these costs. In our next sections, we will delve into the various strategies to create backlinks.

# Legacy approaches to building backlinks
Securing backlinks is critical for SEO success, but the process is far from straightforward. Various methods exist, each with its potential drawbacks. However, one approach stands out, offering a unique and effective way to build these vital connections. Before we delve into it, let's consider the more conventional strategies:

1. **Link swapping** - Exchanging links with friends who own websites. While seemingly easy, this method usually delivers little value, as these websites may not have a high domain authority, limiting their impact.
2. **Guest blogging** - Reaching out to other blogs, offering to write a post in exchange for a backlink. This approach is often seen as a paid engagement.
3. **Buying backlinks** - Purchasing links from high domain authority websites. However, some sites selling backlinks may lack traffic, diminishing their overall value.
4. **Organic growth** - Publishing high-quality content, hoping others find it valuable and link back to it. This method is unpredictable.
5. **Directory listings** - Listing your site on directories can be helpful, but opportunities are eventually exhausted.
6. **Public relations (PR)** - Proposing articles to platforms like TechCrunch can be beneficial, but it often requires a PR agency.
7. **Buying expired domains** - Purchasing domains with existing high domain authority and traffic can be expensive.

While these approaches have challenges, there's an innovative alternative - a platform called **Featured** (previously known as Terkel). This unique tool is revolutionizing the way we build backlinks.
