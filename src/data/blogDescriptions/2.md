### Startup Autobahn: Anticipating Automotive Innovations
I'm really excited about the Startup Autobahn in Stuttgart, Germany, in 2024. This isn't like the other startup conferences; it's a unique innovation platform that bridges the gap between startups and the automotive industry, leading to collaborations that drive the sector forward.&nbsp;

With experts from all facets of the auto industry, cutting-edge tech showcases, and a vibrant space to network, it's an exceptional opportunity to gain insights and connect with potential partners. Plus, it's in Stuttgart, the birthplace of the car, adding a touch of history to the future-focused event. I just can't miss that!


[Abid Salahi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/abid-salahi/), Co-Founder and CEO, [FinlyWealth](https://www.finlywealth.com)



### NOAH Zurich: Tech Meets Environmental Care
I'm really looking forward to the NOAH Conference in Zurich this December. This event brings together leaders in the digital world and now also focuses on taking care of our planet.&nbsp;

At NOAH Zurich, there's a mix of talks from tech companies and those working to protect nature. I think this conference is a great place to learn, meet others, and talk about tech and the environment. It's about making a positive change together.



[Marco Genaro Palma](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marco-genaro-palma/), Content Marketing Manager, [PRLab](https://prlab.co)



### London Tech Week: A Tech Industry Epicenter
London Tech Week is a major event for startups, with dedicated stages for startups and usually several speakers from startups as well as major players like Google. The tech space is especially conducive to innovation and startups, and this European event is the place to learn, network, grow, and impact the tech industry.&nbsp;

Over 30,000 people attend and more than 300 people speak over the event's five days, not to mention numerous exhibitors. In 2024, London Tech Week is expanding with a new location at London Olympia that features twice the amount of space, so it's sure to be the most impactful event yet.



[Thomas Samuels](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thomas-samuels-70709414a/), President, [Cardinal Expo](https://cardinalexpo.com)

First, the European Innovation Summit is renowned for gathering a diverse spectrum of innovative startups, influential industry leaders, and investors. This convergence provides an exceptional platform for networking, fostering partnerships, and exploring new business opportunities. These interactions can potentially open doors to collaborations and clients that are instrumental in our agency's growth.

Second, the summit typically hosts discussions, panels, and presentations on emerging trends and technologies in the startup ecosystem. Staying informed about the latest industry developments is pivotal in ensuring that CodeDesign remains at the forefront of digital marketing strategies and solutions.


[Bruno Gavino](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brunogavino/), Founder, CEO, [CodeDesign](https://codedesign.org)

### VivaTech Paris: Exploring Emerging Tech Solutions
I'm particularly excited about attending VivaTech in Paris from May 22-25, 2024. VivaTech has consistently proven to be a hub of innovation, offering attendees a glimpse into the future of technology and business.

Given our company's mission to aid software buyers in selecting the best tools, the event offers a unique opportunity to explore emerging solutions and engage directly with their creators. ### WordCamp Germany: A Community-Driven Tech Gathering
In the bustling calendar of European conferences, one event that has consistently piqued my interest is WordCamp Germany. While it may not be labeled strictly as a startup conference, its significance in the digital realm must be recognized. Set against the backdrop of Germany's vibrant tech scene, WordCamp offers a unique blend of workshops, discussions, and networking opportunities.

The actual value of WordCamp Germany lies in its community-driven approach. It's a melting pot of developers, designers, entrepreneurs, and digital enthusiasts. For startups, especially those in the digital space, it's an unparalleled opportunity to connect with individuals with the expertise and insights to propel a young business forward. The collaborative atmosphere fosters genuine connections, ensuring attendees gain knowledge and build relationships that can be instrumental in scaling their ventures.



[Shane McEvoy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/seolondon/), MD, [Flycast Media](https://www.flycastmedia.co.uk)

### Perspektywy Summit: Empowering Women in Tech
The Perspektywy Women in Tech Summit 2024, Europe's largest conference and career fair for women in technology, will take place on June 12–13, 2024, at Expo XXI in Warsaw, Poland (also available online).&nbsp;

The previous year's summit drew 11,000 attendees, featured 500 presenters, covered 200 tech topics across four stages, and featured 85 tech companies at career fairs, 300 mentoring sessions, 50 specialized workshops, and 20 side events over the course of two days of inspiration. The event seeks to inspire and empower women in STEM, technology, and information technology by providing a unique opportunity for networking, knowledge development, career advancement, and cooperation.&nbsp;

This year's theme, "Making Bold Choices," aims to influence the future of technology and the world while celebrating and connecting the digital community.


[Cindi Keller](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cindi914/), Communications Coordinator, [The Criminal Defense Firm](https://criminaldefense.com)
