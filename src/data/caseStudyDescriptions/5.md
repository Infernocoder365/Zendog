##### Company

Corza Medical is a private equity-backed healthcare company specializing in surgical technologies and devices. With a strong focus on operational efficiency and growth, Corza supports physicians and healthcare providers with high-quality products.

##### Situation

Corza sought to improve commercial excellence by aligning sales, marketing, and operations across the organization. The goal was to increase win rates, shorten the sales cycle, and enhance team performance and morale.

##### Actions

- Facilitated cross-functional workshops to address sales challenges and process gaps
- Conducted stakeholder interviews to uncover pain points and improvement opportunities
- Developed tactical strategies for sales enablement and account management

##### Results

- Improved sales team morale and collaboration across departments
- Identified and implemented tactical strategies to accelerate deal cycles
- Strengthened team spirit and sense of ownership among leaders

- **Capabilities**: sales strategy, commercial excellence, leadership alignment, workshop facilitation
