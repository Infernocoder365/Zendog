##### Company

SOLO is a FinTech company that helps banks and lenders streamline customer data collection. By automating and verifying first-party data, SOLO reduces the process from months to minutes, enabling lenders to uncover new opportunities. The company operates from New York and Cairo.

##### Situation

Georgina, the founder of SOLO, initially approached us for competitive research and pricing analysis. This quickly evolved into a fractional Head of Growth role. Our focus shifted to finding product-market fit, increasing enterprise sales, and preparing for a $1M seed funding round while securing key pilot customers.

##### Actions

- **Strategy Development:** Worked with Georgina to refine the product direction and go-to-market strategy.

- **Enterprise Sales:** Created custom presentations for top prospects like Amazon, Mastercard, and Upwork, and coached on sales calls.

- **Product Demo:** Scripted and recorded a demo to showcase SOLO’s platform to potential clients.

- **Fundraising:** Built the pitch deck and helped Georgina prepare for investor meetings.

- **Website Overhaul:** Led the redesign and copywriting of SOLO’s website to enhance clarity and user engagement.

- **Agency Management:** Coordinated with web development, PR, and marketing agencies to quickly test new channels.

- **PR Content:** Wrote articles to secure media coverage.

- **Executive Coaching:** Provided ongoing coaching for Georgina on leadership, sales tactics, and investor relations.


##### Results

- Raised $1M in Series Seed funding.

- Secured partnerships with high-profile pilot customers.

- Launched a revamped website, improving lead generation.

- Gained media coverage in major outlets.
