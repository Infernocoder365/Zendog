##### Company

Dante AI is a fast-growing generative AI platform that enables businesses to build custom AI assistants, automate workflows, and enhance customer engagement. The company serves a global customer base across industries.

##### Situation

Dante AI engaged us to accelerate growth through funnel optimization and channel expansion. The goal was to increase qualified sign-ups, improve conversion rates from free to paid, and enhance lifetime value through better onboarding and retention strategies.

##### Actions

- Conducted growth audits across acquisition, activation, and retention
- Developed a prioritized growth roadmap focused on high-impact experiments
- Implemented conversion-focused landing pages and onboarding improvements

##### Results

- Increased monthly sign-ups and improved free-to-paid conversion
- Built a scalable experimentation framework for ongoing growth
- Strengthened positioning and messaging across key customer segments
