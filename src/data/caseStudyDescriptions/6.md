##### Company

Intellivy is a strategy consulting firm and platform that helps companies validate pricing, positioning, and product concepts through consumer research. Focused on actionable insight, Intellivy blends strategic consulting with a self-serve SaaS platform.

##### Situation

Intellivy sought to scale its revenue from $3M to $10M ARR and needed a structured go-to-market strategy to drive predictable growth. The goal was to improve lead generation, conversion rates, and customer onboarding while strengthening brand positioning.

##### Actions

- Reactivated and optimized Google Ads and key SEO pages
- Implemented new persona-driven landing pages and clarified customer messaging
- Simplified and streamlined onboarding flows across touchpoints

##### Results

- Achieved seven-figure ARR with improved growth momentum
- Increased conversion rate through enhanced messaging and landing page optimization
- Better operational efficiency and onboarding experience for new clients

##### Context

- **Capabilities**: go-to-market strategy, growth marketing, SEO/SEM optimization, onboarding optimization

- **Technology**: Google Ads, Google Analytics, WordPress, Notion

- **Locations**: Netherlands

- **Industries**: SaaS, consulting
