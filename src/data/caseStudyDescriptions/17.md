##### Company

Aryo is a North American supply chain and logistics consulting firm with a startup mentality, combining traditional and modern approaches. The firm is dedicated to delivering expert analysis and helping businesses and founders optimize their logistics operations for improved efficiency and effectiveness.

##### Situation

Aryo had established a presence in a few industries and wanted to expand into new markets. They needed help choosing the next 2-3 industries to target and asked for an audit of their marketing and sales tactics. Our role at Zendog Labs was to guide Aryo through a structured process of market segmentation and prioritization while optimizing their go-to-market strategy for future growth.

##### Actions

We addressed four key questions:

1. **Who is Aryo, and what are they best at?** We analyzed Aryo’s core strengths and unique capabilities to identify areas where they could excel in new markets.

2. **Which industries are the best fit for Aryo's growth?** We generated a list of 148 potential industries and used quantitative and qualitative criteria to narrow it down to a few high-potential options.

3. **What are the market dynamics of the shortlisted industries?** For each selected industry, we evaluated market potential, growth prospects, and how well they aligned with Aryo’s strengths.

4. **How effective are Aryo's current marketing and sales efforts?** We audited their marketing and sales tactics, identifying improvement opportunities and recommending strategies to help them succeed in new industries.


##### Results

The engagement led to clear, actionable outcomes:

- A shortlist of three high-priority industries for Aryo’s expansion, narrowed down from an initial list of 148.

- In-depth insights into each shortlisted industry, enabling Aryo to confidently evaluate market potential and strategic fit.

- A comprehensive marketing and sales strategy with actionable steps for both the short term (Phase 1) and long term (Phase 2) to support growth efforts.

- A final presentation delivered within three weeks, providing Aryo with a clear roadmap for expanding into new industries and improving their marketing and sales strategies.


This guidance laid a solid foundation for Aryo to expand into new markets and refine their go-to-market strategy for future growth.
