##### Company

Brightfield Group is a market research and analytics firm focused on the cannabis and wellness industries. They provide data-driven insights to help companies make better strategic decisions, supported by a growing SaaS platform.

##### Situation

The CEO of Brightfield Group sought external coaching support to navigate both strategic and operational decisions while managing a growing team and product line. The goal was to maintain growth momentum, improve organizational communication, and accelerate the development of their SaaS platform.

##### Actions

- Conducted weekly sparring sessions focused on strategic planning and leadership development
- Advised on OKRs, team structure, and leadership priorities
- Supported decision-making around product development and go-to-market strategy

##### Results

- Accelerated development of the SaaS platform and improved focus on high-impact initiatives
- Strengthened leadership communication and alignment across the team
- Enhanced clarity on strategic priorities and execution
