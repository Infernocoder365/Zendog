##### Company

VAMED is a global healthcare group providing planning, construction, and operations services for hospitals and healthcare facilities worldwide. With a workforce of over 20,000 professionals and operations across multiple continents, VAMED is a leader in integrated healthcare solutions.

##### Situation

VAMED senior leadership sought support to define a compelling long-term vision for 2030 that would align the organization and inspire cross-functional collaboration. The goal was to move from a tactical, short-term focus to a strategic, visionary approach that would drive organizational cohesion and performance.

##### Actions

- Conducted interviews with senior leadership and key stakeholders
- Facilitated workshops to align teams and generate strategic insights
- Synthesized findings into a comprehensive 2030 Vision document

##### Results

- Enhanced team cohesion and cross-functional collaboration
- Shifted mindsets from tactical to strategic, enabling long-term planning
- Delivered a clear and compelling strategic vision for the organization
